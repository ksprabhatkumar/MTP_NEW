OTHER APPROACHES FOR PLACEMENT-ROBUST WEARABLE HAR
Research review: 17 November 2026
Proposals only. T08 OPEN. No training authorization; no expected accuracy gain is guaranteed.

1. RECOMMENDATION

PRIMARY: bounded physical displacement augmentation plus source-only worst-group learning on the robust NO-GRAPH selective SSM (A). Separate coordinate rotation from position shift, and ask what residual failure remains after invariance. Graph routing is a controlled experimental factor, not a required winner.
BACKUP: compact multi-lag within-IMU geometry with an optional signed-volume feature (B). This is easier and cheaper; its novelty is weaker and needs an information-preservation/efficiency argument plus strong comparisons.

C (learned equivariant representation) is a strong comparator and a possible higher-cost direction. D (dual experts), E (donor-utility graph correction), F (self-supervised temporal geometry) are secondary options. Do not run every family merely because it is plausible.

Direct prior-art constraints:
- TRI-HAR (ISWC2026) already combines per-location equivariance/invariance with REALDISP mutual-displacement evaluation [R1]. Neither invariant multi-IMU HAR nor testing it on real displacement is new.
- MODA (CVPR2025) studies motion-drift augmentation [R2], and WIMUSim realistic IMU variability [R3]. Physics-aware augmentation is not itself new.
- Masked reconstruction plus Siamese/temporal rotation consistency has already been studied [R9].
- Dynamic interactions, SSM-related wearable fusion and sensor transferability weighting are established in DynamicWHAR, DecomposeWHAR and SALIENCE [R6-R8].

A defensible primary hypothesis is narrower: after removing fixed mounting rotations through invariance, does a bounded same-segment position-shift model with source-defined worst-group training improve remaining real-placement errors beyond generic gain/drift, rotation augmentation and published motion-drift augmentation, under matched capacity and compute? This is a candidate distinction, not established novelty. A detailed comparison with MODA/WIMUSim may invalidate it. A new method name or replacing a temporal module with Mamba is insufficient. No finite search proves absence of prior art.

3. GEOMETRY AND DATA CONTRACT

Audited raw windows: [T,N,F]=[50,9,6], stride25; declared rate50Hz. Six channels are acceleration a and angular velocity w. Subject, recording, scenario, window-start and availability metadata exist. Induced subjects are[2,5,15]. Retain the three known invalid all-zero-file exclusions in O2Corpus.

Current five-channel mag+dot is exactly
 u_i(t)=[||a_t||,||w_t||,a_t^T w_t,a_t^T a_(t-1),w_t^T w_(t-1)].
The first lag uses the first sample itself. This is not a full Gram representation. If the same fixed proper rotation R_i acts on both modalities throughout a window, u(R_i X_i)=u(X_i). Time-varying rotations generally change lag terms. Transform raw vectors before feature normalization; fit scalar statistics on training subjects only.

Independent mounting rotations form SO(3)^9. Cross-sensor vector dot products are not invariant to independent R_i and R_j. Fuse vectors only after a valid shared-frame alignment, or fuse invariant scalars. A no-graph temporal/classifier model can still mix sensors: no graph does not mean no cross-sensor information.

Orientation offsets, translation on one rigid segment, soft-tissue motion, arbitrary body-site relocation, dropout and channel permutation are different interventions. No exact per-sensor displacement vector or time-aligned ideal/induced trajectory was established in the processed metadata reviewed. Do not train a physical displacement regressor from scenario labels, or treat same-activity recordings as aligned motion pairs.

4. APPROACH A: BOUNDED PHYSICAL DISPLACEMENT + WORST-GROUP LEARNING
Rank1; primary; works with graph or no graph.

Idea/mathematics (proposed adaptation of established mechanics): for a point offset r fixed on the SAME rigid segment, expressed in the original sensor frame,
 delta a(t,r)=alpha(t) cross r + w(t) cross (w(t) cross r).
With a constant new mounting rotation Q:
 a'(t)=Q[a(t)+delta a(t,r)], w'(t)=Q w(t).
Gravity is approximately equal at nearby points and cancels in the difference. Convert gyro to radians/s and acceleration to SI units before calculating. Estimate alpha=dw/dt using a fixed smoothing/derivative operator. Constant r excludes relative-motion/Coriolis terms; the model does not describe a loose strap or crossing a joint.

Define training groups g by source subject or synthetic intervention family/severity. Start with coarse family groups to avoid sparse subject-by-class cells. Use
 L=L_clean+lambda sum_g q_g L_g,
 q_g <- q_g exp(eta L_g), normalized to sum1.
This is a group-DRO-style rule [R4]. Compare ERM with identical augmented samples. Class-balanced source sampling may be necessary so hard activities are not confused with nuisance severity. Group weights and all parameters use training/source validation only.

Why it may help: fixed rotation invariance does not remove acceleration changes due to position. Gyro may provide relatively position-stable motion on a rigid segment. Worst-group learning may prevent easy subjects dominating the average. It can also amplify noisy labels or sparse classes.

Prior art/distinction: MODA[R2], WIMUSim[R3], rigid-part position-invariant recognition[R12] and DRO[R4] directly limit novelty. Candidate gap: source-only bounded position stress AFTER invariant encoding, with explicit rotation-versus-position and ERM-versus-DRO controls. Claim novelty only after full algorithm comparison; a small implementation is a feasibility choice, not a new scientific principle.

Integration: new augmentation module before o2_features.features; CPU-staged raw windows; same five-channel SSM baseline initially. Estimate derivatives inside recording boundaries with valid context masks. Preserve sensor identity and availability. Put changes in a new experiment namespace; do not edit an active T10 run's snapshotted scientific source.

Controls/ablations: clean ERM, rotation only, current gain/drift, offset only, rotation+offset, same samples with ERM/DRO; acceleration-only, gyro-only and combined; r=0/Q=I sanity cases; derivative smoothing and source-defined severity; matched local capacity and one graph arm after no-graph mechanism evidence. Reproduce MODA faithfully; if WIMUSim needs unavailable calibration/kinematics, label the comparison limitation rather than inventing its inputs.

Risks: incorrect units/frame sign, noisy derivatives, physically implausible offsets, saturation or label-changing severe stress. No tuning r on subject15 induced outcomes. Real soft-tissue shifts may violate the model. Rotating by a time-varying Q(t) without modelling its additional angular motion is not a physically valid loose-mount simulation.

Paper evidence: improvement beyond matched generic and published augmentation, stable subject-level recorded-placement gains, retained ideal performance, sensitivity curves and new independent confirmation. If only synthetic rotation improves, reject the physical-position claim. If DRO adds nothing, remove that claim.

Estimated implementation2–4developer days. Per16-epoch robust no-graph SSM job: laptop0.45–0.90h; workstation conditional scenario0.15–0.90h (Section10). No new runtime was measured.

5. APPROACH B: COMPACT MULTI-LAG GEOMETRY
Rank2; backup; no graph required.

Let v,u be acceleration or gyro, and L a small predeclared lag set (e.g.{1,2,4} is a proposal, not a tuned choice). Add
 G_(v,u,l)(t)=v(t)^T u(t-l),
 C=G/[(||v(t)||+epsilon)(||u(t-l)||+epsilon)].
Retain magnitude channels to preserve intensity. Differences ||v_t-v_(t-l)|| are determined by norms/dots: they are a reparameterization control, not extra information.

Optional signed volume:
 V_l(t)=a_t^T[a_(t-l) cross a_(t-2l)], with an all-gyro analogue.
It is invariant under det(R)=+1 and can distinguish signed geometry lost by ordinary same-vector Gram features under abstract reflection. Gyro is an axial vector: do not claim physical mirror invariance by treating acceleration and gyro identically under improper rotations. The guarantee is SO(3). Signed volumes may be nearly zero for planar motion or noise-sensitive. Mask unsupported prefixes instead of inventing preceding samples.

Why: preserve temporal motion geometry discarded by five scalars. Limits: absolute orientation is still lost; translation and within-window mounting changes remain. More features may simply add capacity or redundant noise.

Prior art/gap: geometric/SVD invariants[R5], learned local invariants[R1], temporal Gram consistency[R9], and our lag-one features. Candidate distinction is a small information-audited feature family with a measured accuracy/efficiency trade-off. More dots alone is weak novelty.

Integration: new feature mode with explicit width p and p->d projection; version WIDTHS/config/preprocessing. Parameter-match the changed first layer. Keep T50 and SSM dimensions unchanged initially; no dense100x100 Gram matrix per IMU.

Controls: magnitude, mag+dot, temporal lags only, cross-modal lags, normalized/unnormalized products, signed term on/off, capacity-matched scalar controls, geometric/SVD preprocessing and TRI-HAR-like encoder. Test fixed independent rotations, time-varying rotations and real placement separately. Report low-motion numerical stability and affected classes.

Risks: lag caches crossing recording/split boundaries, test-derived normalization, loss of informative posture axes, and induced-driven lag selection. Compute lag support consistently across controls.

Paper evidence: useful new temporal information beyond lag-one, fair learned-invariant comparisons, real displacement and efficiency. If it merely matches existing approaches, frame it as a compact baseline/benchmark contribution subject to venue expectations.

Estimated1–2developer days;0.45–0.65laptop h/job; conditional workstation0.15–0.65h. Four variants x four source fold/seed pairs=16jobs,7.2–10.4laptop h before reserve, minus exactly compatible anchors.


6. APPROACH C: LEARNED PER-IMU EQUIVARIANT ENCODER BEFORE SCALAR SSM
Rank3; expensive comparator/conditional research direction.

Maintain vector channels V_i(t) in R^(c x3). Linear mixing W V commutes with right multiplication by R^T. Use equivariant nonlinearities/normalization, then norms or inner products:
 E(R_i X_i)=E(X_i)R_i^T,
 z_i=Invariant(E(X_i)), p=Classifier(SSM(Fuse(z_1,...,z_9))).
Coordinate-wise ReLU generally breaks equivariance. Independent invariance must precede cross-IMU vector fusion. Isotropic modality scaling is required before vector layers.

Why: learned geometry may retain more class information than handcrafted scalars. Limits: larger activations, no position-shift guarantee, discarded absolute orientation, and possible overcapacity for T50. It is not invariant to arbitrary time-varying rotations without further assumptions.

Prior art: Vector Neurons[R10] supplies the toolbox; TRI-HAR[R1] is a direct wearable method with recorded-displacement evidence. A straightforward reproduction is a comparator, not our novelty. An uncertainty-aware bottleneck or a carefully justified temporal integration would need its own precise mechanism; simply replacing its backbone with Mamba is weak distinction.

Integration: shared per-sensor vector encoder -> invariant scalar features -> fixed-width projection -> verified SSM. Keep nominal sensor identity and availability. Reuse T09 tests only for unchanged SSM code, and add numerical full-path invariance checks. Prototype small width before assuming4GB fits.

Controls: author TRI-HAR, minimal VN, mag+dot, B, joint-global versus per-location fusion, scalar parameter/FLOP controls, no/fixed/dynamic sensor fusion. Same six channels, splits, training budget. Original paper scores remain separate from reimplemented project-protocol scores.

Risks: hidden coordinate-wise normalization, cross-IMU vector mixing, external preprocessing differences, and extra capacity posing as invariance gain. No magnetometer/quaternion advantage unless comparator inputs match.

Evidence: representation benefit beyond both handcrafted and published learned invariants, accurate invariance proof/test, real-placement evaluation and training/inference cost. Estimated3–6developer days;0.9–2.3laptop h per16-epoch job, conditional workstation0.3–2.3h. Memory fit is unverified; workstation preferred for this family.

7. APPROACH D: INVARIANT / AXIS-SENSITIVE EXPERT MIXTURE
Rank4; conditional on a persistent clean-versus-shift trade-off.

Train invariant expert p_I and raw-axis expert p_A. Combine probabilities:
 p(y|X)=g(z(X))p_I(y|X)+(1-g(z(X)))p_A(y|X), 0<=g<=1.
Train the gate using out-of-fold SOURCE predictions and mixture CE. Features can include disagreement, source-calibrated uncertainty, rotation sensitivity and cross-sensor consistency. Gate inputs must not include test scenario labels. Entropy alone is not a displacement detector; confident errors exist.

Why: preserve useful ideal posture/orientation information while retaining a robust expert. Limits: mixture usually loses exact invariance; gate may recognize activity/subject/recording signatures rather than misplacement. A synthetic quality label is not a physical-placement label.

Prior art: SALIENCE[R8] already uses sensor transferability attention with target adaptation; inconsistent-wearable work used invariant substitute classifiers[R13]. Conditional fusion is established. Candidate distinction is source-only, recording-separated gate calibration and reliable risk/coverage evidence, not first adaptive HAR.

Integration: start with frozen compatible checkpoints, cache logits keyed by corpus index/recording and train a small gate. A new raw/SSM expert is needed if existing raw checkpoints have another recipe. Each gate-training example needs predictions from experts that did not train on that subject. Existing pilot checkpoints are not automatically OOF for all source subjects.

Controls: each expert, equal mixture, source-tuned constant mixture, learned gate, simple logistic stacking, a larger single-model capacity control, and explicitly labelled ORACLE routing as a bound. Ablate uncertainty cues. Evaluate NLL/Brier/ECE, false alarms by activity, selective risk and coverage. Abstained windows cannot vanish from the accuracy denominator without disclosure.

Risks: OOF leakage, mismatched class ordering, gate collapse, unseen scenario confidence shifts. Accurate activity predictions do not establish localization of the misplaced sensor. Such a claim needs per-sensor ground truth not established in processed metadata.

Evidence: improved ideal/shift trade-off versus each expert and simple averaging, robust source-only gating on unseen users, calibration and deployment overhead. Estimated2–4developer days; gate fit minutes to1h once caches exist; paired expert training0.7–1.5laptop h/fold/seed, conditional workstation0.23–1.5h, BEFORE OOF fold multiplication. Inference generally requires two expert passes.

8. APPROACH E: CROSS-FITTED DONOR-UTILITY GRAPH CORRECTION
Rank5; graph hypothesis, lower priority after the failed utility gate.

Keep robust local path h_i. Let m_ij=P(h_j,e_ij), j!=i, be receiver-blind donor predictions. Form
 a_ij=softmax_j(b_ij-tau*s_ij),
 h'_i=h_i+g_i sum_(j!=i) a_ij m_ij, 0<=g_i<=g_max.
Include an explicit null donor. s_ij is invariant inconsistency/uncertainty, not raw-axis disagreement. Cross-fit teachers on source subjects to create a utility target: whether the donor decreases classification loss relative to the local-only path on held-out SOURCE examples. Stop gradients through targets. Do not train the utility head only on overfit teacher predictions for its own training data.

Why: judge corrective usefulness instead of correlation. Limits: mutually wrong sensors can agree; complementary limb motions need not agree; useful edges need not be anatomical. Another gate with the same failed target is not a meaningful new method.

Prior art: DynamicWHAR[R6], DecomposeWHAR[R7], SALIENCE[R8], plus our predictive graph and failed utility gate. Candidate distinction is cross-fitted utility targets with causal donor interventions. Attention, residual correction and gating themselves are established.

Integration: new predictive-model branch, frozen baseline first, invariant scalar donor summaries, identical local virtual-slot capacity control. Preserve zero-correction initialization and numerical equivalence tolerances. Keep SSM as a matched independent factor.

Controls: no graph, local capacity, old predictive graph, ungated graph, previous utility gate, fixed/random edges, null-only, degree/parameter controls; remove/shuffle donors. Temporal donor tests must exclude overlapping windows, match availability and evaluate the same eligible receiver subset. Same-activity donor labels are diagnostic only, never deployment inputs. Permutations are repeated interventions, not independent subjects.

Risks: cross-fitting teacher contamination, synthetic utility not transferring to real placement, graph smoothing away local evidence, and post-hoc selection of favorable donor subsets. Report eligibility and per-subject sign, including negative outcomes.

Evidence: graph exceeds strong no graph AND local capacity on a frozen real-placement protocol; removing truly informative donors removes its advantage. If not, reject the graph mechanism. Estimated3–5developer days;0.8–1.5laptop h per16-epoch graph job, conditional workstation0.27–1.5h, plus teacher cross-fitting and donor-audit evaluation. No improvement is promised.

9. APPROACH F: SOURCE-ONLY SELF-SUPERVISED TEMPORAL GEOMETRY
Rank6 for deadline feasibility; useful if label efficiency is central.

Two source views X,T(X), student f and stop-gradient teacher h:
 L=L_masked_invariant+lambda||q(f(T(X)))-stopgrad(h(X))||^2
   +mu*L_temporal_structure+nu*L_variance.
Predict invariant masked geometry rather than arbitrary raw xyz from an invariant bottleneck; alternatively condition a raw decoder on the known applied rotation. Mask contiguous spans to avoid trivial interpolation. Use a validated non-collapse mechanism. Applying fixed rotation to already invariant mag+dot gives the same input: consistency alone can be redundant, not a new learning signal.

Why: exploit unlabeled SOURCE recordings and preserve dynamics beyond class labels. Limits: pretraining can learn subject or recording identity, and cannot recover information discarded by the representation.

Prior art: TS2Vec[R11] establishes time-series representation learning; recent Siamese masked reconstruction and rotation/temporal consistency[R9] directly overlaps this objective. Displacement-specific physically valid views plus an invariant target and rigorously separated evaluation are a possible distinction, pending novelty review. Adding SSM is insufficient alone.

Integration: pretrain encoder/SSM per outer training split; supervise fine-tuning with a frozen budget. No unlabeled held-out subjects unless a separately labelled transductive protocol is intended. A model pretrained globally across all users is not valid source-only LOSO. Keep external pretraining datasets and overlap checks explicit.

Controls: from scratch, longer supervised compute-matched training, masked-only, consistency-only, combined, TS2Vec, R9, label-budget curves, linear probe versus full fine-tuning. Avoid overlapping neighboring windows as independent contrastive examples; same-activity negatives may be false negatives.

Risks: recording shortcuts, augmentation destroying labels, test-user pretraining leakage, reconstruction of nuisance axes, and extra training compute driving gains. Evidence needs label-efficiency curves and matched-compute comparisons plus real-placement generalization.

Estimated3–6developer days. Illustrative50pretrain epochs+16fine-tuning epochs:2–6laptop h/fold/seed; conditional workstation0.67–6h. This epoch count is a costing scenario, not an approved setting. Multiple views and fold-specific pretraining favor workstation memory.

11. EVALUATION, LEAKAGE AND REUSE CONTRACT

Data separation:
Use source-subject ideal recordings for fitting. Current source screen excludes subjects2/5, validates3/8, seeds13/29. That is two validation subjects, not four independent people. Planned final folds2/5/15 and seeds13/29/47 remain required where scientifically compatible, but their induced data are already inspected. A new seed is not independent confirmation; nested validation cannot undo earlier human decisions informed by target results.

For a stronger paper obtain a locked new displaced cohort or genuinely untouched recordings/subjects with a documented inspection history. A second dataset with comparable RECORDED shifts is valuable. Ordinary DSADS/PAMAP2 plus synthetic rotations tests transfer/invariance, not independent physical displacement. If new data are unavailable, clearly state retrospective exploratory REALDISP evidence instead of claiming untouched confirmation.

Split by subject/recording before overlapping windows. For temporal splits purge at least the full raw support of windows plus lag/derivative/pretraining context. T50/stride25 means adjacent windows share half the samples. No random window splits, overlapping donor retrieval, test-derived feature statistics, or test-user BatchNorm adaptation in the source-only protocol. Cache recording metadata and fingerprints; keep unavailable/all-zero inputs masked consistently.

Matched comparisons:
Hold backbone, class set, channels, optimizer, precision, batch size, training steps and validation fixed. Parameter/compute-match extra modules. Extra feature channels change the first layer. Published model scores from other splits/modalities are not directly comparable. If external reproduction is impossible, say so and narrow the claim rather than presenting a surrogate as the original method.

Checkpoint selection: source ideal fixed33 macro-F1 as in T10. Predeclare candidate ranking on source self and clean-deficit tolerance; inherited0.5pp ideal deficit is a reasonable screening constraint, not a significance threshold. Never select on induced results. Freeze the candidate count/stopping rule before expensive evaluation. Do not search indefinitely until a graph wins.

Metrics: fixed33 macro-F1 and accuracy per subject/scenario; class support/per-class F1/confusions; paired seed-averaged subject deltas; ideal-to-shift degradation; coverage/missing-sensor records; calibration; latency, memory, parameters and total compute. Common-class metrics are supplementary and must be labelled. Separate sensor dropout, rotations and relocation.

Only three induced subjects make uncertainty fragile. Seeds/windows are not independent population units. Show every subject and sign; do not infer a tiny population p-value from overlapping windows. Subject/recording hierarchical bootstrap may be descriptive with an explicit small-sample caveat. Independent participants provide stronger evidence than more seeds. Report all exploratory candidate comparisons and selection history.

Reuse ledger:
- Historical O2 predictions/summaries: reuse for descriptive figures, class errors, baseline context and negative evidence. They are matched controls only if data/source/config/selection fingerprints match the new study. Another SSM recipe usually needs new matched training.
- T09 correctness/reference evidence: reusable for unchanged component hashes. New frontend geometry still needs its own tests.
- Completed T10 jobs: preserve and reuse as mag+dot anchors when scientifically compatible. They lack historical actual-stress hashes; a later probe cannot recreate that proof. Do not automatically discard or retrain them, and do not claim the missing historical property verified.
- T08 frozen checkpoints: useful for donor/gate mechanism analysis and suitable expert diagnostics; not evidence for a new untrained representation. No warm-start across folds that introduces a held-out user through another fold's training.
- Failed attempts remain engineering evidence; never include smoke in scientific averages. Active queues are separate from this proposal.
